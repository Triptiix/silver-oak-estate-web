# Open Decisions

The following business and operational decisions require final confirmation before or immediately after launch. They do not block the architectural blueprint but affect public-facing copy and future features.

1. **Final Cancellation Policy Text:** Current working documents contain contradictory statements regarding refunds. The copy needs to be finalized before public launch. (Architecture supports partial/full refunds based on admin action).
2. **Late Checkout Policy:** A standard 10:00 AM checkout with an 11:00 AM check-in leaves a 1-hour buffer. Exact penalties or allowances for late checkout are pending business owner confirmation.
3. **Public Holiday Pricing Rates:** While structural support exists for special-date pricing overrides in the database, the specific rates for major holidays (e.g., Diwali, New Year) are not required for the MVP launch but must be decided later.
4. **Merchant Account Ownership:** Whether the merchant payment gateway is registered under "Rivaan Yadav" or the "Silver Oak Estate" business entity is pending KYC finalization.
