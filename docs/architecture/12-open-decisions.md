# Open Decisions

The following business, legal and operational decisions require explicit approval. They do not change the verified technical architecture, but several block public paid bookings and final legal publication.

## Verified facts

- Varun Yadav owns the Silver Oak Estate property.
- Arpit Chaudhary manages operations, maintenance and on-ground organization.
- Ownership and operational responsibility do not by themselves determine the legal contracting party, invoice issuer, tax registrant or payment-gateway merchant.

## Launch-blocking decisions

1. **Legal contracting party and business disclosure:** Confirm the person or registered entity that enters into bookings with guests, receives contractual notices and appears in mandatory business disclosures.
2. **Invoice and receipt authority:** Confirm who issues booking receipts or invoices and which name, address, tax identifiers and contact details may be displayed.
3. **Payment-gateway merchant ownership:** Confirm the exact Razorpay account holder or entity through KYC evidence. Do not infer this from the property owner, operator or website account holder.
4. **Cancellation, refund, rescheduling and no-show terms:** Approve exact timelines, deductions, refund method, exceptional-event treatment and administrative discretion.
5. **Damage and security deposit:** Confirm whether a deposit applies, the amount or calculation method, inspection process, deduction evidence and return timeline.
6. **Event liability and vendor responsibility:** Confirm guest, organizer and external-vendor obligations, prohibited activities, indemnity wording and required permissions.
7. **Photography and media consent:** Confirm opt-in/opt-out treatment for guest images, event media and promotional reuse.
8. **Privacy and guest-data treatment:** Approve retention periods, booking-data purposes, sharing, access/correction requests and contact details for privacy enquiries.
9. **Tax and GST disclosure:** Confirm current registration and charging position with qualified accounting advice before accepting live payments.
10. **Dispute resolution and jurisdiction:** Approve governing law, courts or alternative-dispute process and notice procedure.

## Non-blocking or post-launch decisions

1. **Late checkout policy:** A standard 10:00 AM checkout with an 11:00 AM check-in leaves a one-hour operational buffer. Exact allowances and charges remain pending.
2. **Public-holiday pricing:** The database supports special-date overrides, but exact holiday pricing can remain deferred until approved.
3. **OTA/PMS adoption:** Direct and assisted bookings remain the launch model; deeper channel-manager integration is deferred.
